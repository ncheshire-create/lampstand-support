FAQ

How does the streak work?

Answer the day's first question, right or wrong, and your lamp is lit. One grace day each week keeps your streak alive if you miss a day.

Where do the questions come from?

They are original questions that follow the weekly Come, Follow Me reading schedule and cite the verses they come from.

Is Lampstand free? Does it collect data?

Free, with no account, ads or tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Setup and tap Reset progress.

I spotted a wrong answer.

Please email us the question and the verse and we will fix it.

Is this an official church app?

No. Lampstand is an unofficial study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any church.
