Lampstand

Privacy Policy · Zbyt Studios

Summary

Lampstand does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.

Data on your device

Your streak, answers, review list and reminder time are saved only on your device and are removed when you reset progress or delete the app.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

No accounts, ads or tracking

Lampstand has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
